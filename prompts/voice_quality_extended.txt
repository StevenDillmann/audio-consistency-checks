INTRODUCTION

You are contributing to a benchmark designed to evaluate audio-language models on their ability to understand how something is said, not just what is said.
This benchmark consists of ambiguity-resolution tasks that require models to use vocal characteristics — such as whispering, shouting, monotone delivery, vocal strain, or emotional voice — to choose the correct follow-up sentence among three plausible options.
A text-only model should find all follow-ups equally likely, while an audio-aware model should resolve the ambiguity based on vocal effort, emotional tone, or physiological state expressed through voice.

CATEGORY CONTEXT

Category: Voice Quality and Effort

Task Description: The interpretation of the sentence depends on nonverbal vocal traits such as whispering, shouting, monotone delivery, breathiness, strain, crying, laughing, or trembling. These vocal qualities express emotion, speaker state, or interpersonal dynamics and cannot be inferred from text alone.

Setting: <<SETTING>>

REQUIREMENTS FOR THIS TEST CASE
1. Input Sentence

Length: 4–12 words

Same exact wording for all interpretations

The meaning must shift depending on vocal delivery, not lexical stress or phrase grouping

The sentence must support three different interpretations via distinct voice qualities (e.g., whispered, shouted, crying)

2. Follow-up Sentences (3 total)

Each 5–12 words

Textually plausible in the given setting

Each makes sense under one specific vocal delivery

Each must become clearly incoherent, unlikely, or misleading under the wrong vocal quality

3. Prosody Instructions

For each option:

Provide input prosody instruction: describe how the sentence should be spoken (e.g., whispered, hoarse, shouted, monotone, trembling)

Provide follow-up prosody instruction: describe how the continuation should sound to match/respond to that delivery

Use imperative style, describing intensity, vocal tract tension, airflow, rhythm, or emotional tone

4. Explanations

Use this standardized structure for all three:

"explanations": [
  "Explain why delivering the input in a <<VOICE_QUALITY1>> voice makes this follow-up the correct continuation.",
  "Explain why delivering the input in a <<VOICE_QUALITY2>> voice makes this follow-up the correct continuation.",
  "Explain why delivering the input in a <<VOICE_QUALITY3>> voice makes this follow-up the correct continuation."
]


Each explanation should link the acoustic delivery to the social or emotional intent.

5. Voice Assignment

Set:

"different_voices": true if it makes more sense as a dialogue (e.g., urgent call, whispered warning + reply)

"different_voices": false if the speaker would naturally continue themselves
